## The challenge

A large enterprise platform needed to serve **10+ companies and 50+ divisions** without fragmenting governance, permissions, content, or release workflows. The architecture also needed to connect Salesforce data and operate reliably on Acquia Cloud.

## Architecture approach

- Defined Drupal 11 multisite boundaries and reusable module responsibilities.
- Designed RBAC boundaries for users operating across multiple organizations and roles.
- Established Salesforce synchronization and REST API integration patterns.
- Led the PHP 8.3 and Drupal 11 modernization strategy.
- Standardized deployment automation, observability, and performance practices on Acquia Cloud.

## Outcome

The resulting platform established a scalable foundation for dozens of divisions while preserving clear ownership, secure access, and consistent delivery standards. Performance tuning and modernization strengthened response times and long-term maintainability.

## Core stack

**PHP 8.3 · Drupal 11 · Acquia Cloud · MySQL · Salesforce APIs · RBAC · REST APIs**
